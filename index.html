The Concept

The Feminine Sensuality to a Masculine Legacy

The Queenswoodz @ Bukit Jalil is EXSIM Jalil Link Sdn. Bhd.'s serviced residence, rising as two towers — Tower A and Tower B — above the established and ever-growing township of Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur. Its architecture is a study in contrast: flowing curves meeting strong vertical lines, softness held within structure.

Natural stone, warm wood and brushed metal are layered in warm tones and curated textures, drawing natural light across every corridor and unit. The result is a home that is rooted in strength, yet crowned in elegance — a legacy building designed to feel both monumental and intimate, in the heart of one of Kuala Lumpur's most connected townships.

Location & Connectivity

Positioned at the Heart of Bukit Jalil

The Queenswoodz sits within Bukit Jalil, one of Kuala Lumpur's most established and premium townships — moments from Pavilion Bukit Jalil, framed by the green expanse of Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort and Bukit Jalil Recreational Park, with direct highway and rail access into the KL city centre and beyond.

LRT Awam Besar

900m away via a covered walkway, with direct rail access into the Klang Valley network.

Pavilion Bukit Jalil

Approximately 3 minutes' drive, with proposed bus service to and from LRT Awam Besar.

Novel Park

A 1.47-acre landscaped park right at the doorstep.

Bukit Jalil Recreational Park

1km away, with trails, lakes and open green space for everyday recreation.

Bukit Jalil Golf & Country Resort

Green fairways and clubhouse living within the same established township.

Highway Network

Direct access via Lebuhraya Shah Alam and Lebuhraya KL–Putrajaya, connecting to KL city, KLIA and beyond.
