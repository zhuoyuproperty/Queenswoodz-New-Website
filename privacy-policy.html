Your information is used by Zhuo Yu Property Enterprise and Ng Chow Yit (Joey Ng), a Registered Real Estate Negotiator (REN 85309) with IQI Realty Sdn. Bhd., for the purposes above. Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time. To do so, email zhuoyuproperty@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.

Cookies & analytics

This website may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.
